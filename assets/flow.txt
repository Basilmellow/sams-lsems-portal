## EMS-PD Interaction

This is a huge missing section.

Examples

### Active Shootout

EMS stages nearby.

Do NOT enter until scene declared Code 4.

PD escorts EMS.

Treat officers first if requested by Incident Commander.

---

### Active Hostage

Remain at staging.

Maintain radio silence unless contacted.

Never negotiate.

---

### Traffic Stop

Never stop inside the active stop.

Wait until officer requests assistance.

---

### Crime Scene

Do not destroy evidence.

Only move objects if required to save life.

Inform PD of everything touched.

---

### Prison

Treat inmates equally.

Never uncuff.

Remain with correction staff.

---

### SWAT

Follow Tactical Commander.

Remain behind shield line.
